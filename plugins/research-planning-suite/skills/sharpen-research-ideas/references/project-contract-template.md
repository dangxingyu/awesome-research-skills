# Project contract template

Copy the skeleton below into `contract.md` and fill every section. Angle
brackets are guidance; delete them. Do not leave empty sections — write
`none` deliberately when a section is genuinely empty.

```markdown
# <project title>

## Claim
<one sentence; falsifiable; no hedging conjunctions>

## Kernel
<the user's original itch, near-verbatim; narrowing must preserve this>

## Setting
<objects, models, data, and scale; the smallest setting that can express
the claim>

## Central hypothesis
<the falsifiable statement the stage-1 experiment attacks first>

## Falsification
<the concrete observation that would refute the claim>

## Success criteria
<what counts as done: effect size, proof, benchmark result, or artifact>

## Scope and non-goals
<what is deliberately excluded; adjacent questions not being answered>

## Novelty and nearest neighbors
<2-5 nearest works, each labeled verified / suspected / unknown; no novelty
assertion without a search behind it>

## Resources
<compute, data, and time actually available, from the interview or stated
as an assumption>

## Assumptions made without user input
<autonomous mode: every choice made in place of an interview answer, most
load-bearing first>

## Links
kernel.md · candidates.md · predictions.md · plan.md
```
